⚠️ Input looks unusual RULE-BASED CHECK

The AI classifier reads the satellite image only - SST and wind shear never change the predicted category. They now feed a separate, clearly-labelled rule-based environment check (see "Rapid Intensification" below) - a documented meteorological rule of thumb, not a model. Humidity and vorticity are still kept for a possible future ERA5 extension and do not change anything yet.
